# METRIQ Legal Metrology Knowledge Base

Primary authority: Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India.

Source:
https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/legal-metrology-act

The Department page lists the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 and amendments, including 2025 and 2026 amendments. The application exposes `/api/rules` so the UI can display the rule source.

Every result stores a rule ID, rule number, status, confidence and OCR evidence. This is decision support, not a legal opinion. Review the current official notification and product-specific exceptions before relying on a result.
